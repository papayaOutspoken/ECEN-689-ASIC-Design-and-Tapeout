C2. Why are thousands of clock buffers needed?
The clock must reach thousands of flip-flops distributed across the physical design. Clock buffers divide the load, control transition time and fanout, and balance clock arrival times to reduce skew.

C4. What is an antenna violation, and how does an antenna diode help?
During fabrication, charge can accumulate on a partially formed metal wire connected to a transistor gate and damage its thin gate oxide. An antenna diode provides a discharge path for the accumulated charge and protects the gate.
