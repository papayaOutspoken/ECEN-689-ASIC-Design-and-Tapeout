C2 Why does one clock need thousands of buffers? (2 sentences, own words):
The clock net drives 105,249 flip-flops ([INFO CTS-0010] Clock net "clk" has 105249 sinks.).
Since a single clock driver cannot charge the pin and wire capacitance of that many sinks fast enough to keep the clock slew within its limit, 
TritonCTS built a balanced tree of 13,182 buffers in which each buffer drives only a few loads, so the clock arrives at every flip-flop at nearly the same time.

C4 What is an antenna violation + how does a diode fix it? (2 sentences):
During plasma etching, a long exposed metal line acts as a capacitive antenna that collects charge from the plasma ions, 
and if that line is connected only to the high-impedance gate oxide of a transistor, the charge can discharge through and damage the oxide, 
which Sky130 checks with the Maximum Exposed Gate Area Ratio (MAX_EGAR).
A diode fixes this because the tool inserts a reverse-biased antenna diode (sky130_fd_sc_hd__diode_2) on the net, 
which provides a low-impedance shunt path that bleeds the plasma charge safely into the substrate before it reaches the gate, 
while staying reverse biased and not affecting the circuit in normal operation.
