B2 FINAL CONFIG CHOICE + 3-sentence justification (cycles vs. area vs. risk):
All four configurations we simulated (S 16/16, M 32/32, L 64/64, and asymmetric 64/16) gave identical results, `BENCHMARK PASS checksum=500 cycles=419`, because the entire benchmark loop sits at addresses 0x00–0x24, inside a single cache line, so every fetch is a `cache hit` at `idx:00` and cache size cannot affect the cycle count. With cycles giving no basis for choosing, we decided on area and risk: our Lab 8 synthesis measured M at 4,765,381 µm², under the 5,800,000 limit with about 18% headroom, while L doubles the cache storage that already makes up about 74% of the area and was never guaranteed to fit. We chose M over S because it fits with measured margin while keeping larger caches for real programs that exceed one cache line, and S remains our fallback if placement or Lab 10’s routing shows we need more room.
